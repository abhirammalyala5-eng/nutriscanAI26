How NutriScanAI works

A four-stage pipeline from photos to macronutrients.

1

Dual-angle capture

An overhead photo captures the food footprint and plate rim. A side photo captures mound depth and elevation.

2

Food recognition

A visual segmentation model identifies the plate boundary and classifies the food regions in the overhead view.

3

Portion weight estimation

The plate diameter maps pixels to centimetres. Combined with food density references, this produces gram estimates per component.

4

Nutrition calculation

Regression models calculate Calories, Carbohydrates, Protein, Fats, and Dietary Fibre for each identified component.
