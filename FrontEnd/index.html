How NutraVision Works

An end-to-end multi-stage pipeline designed for offline and edge deployment.

1

Paired 3D Capture

Top-down view captures horizontal food footprint & plate rim; side view captures height elevation profile.

2

Vision & VLM Reasoning

DeepLabV3+ segments 104 food classes while Qwen3-VL (2B) identifies authentic culinary components without hardcoded rules.

3

Calibrated Density Geometry

Combines circular rim scale calibration with empirical IIIT Hyderabad portion bounds to prevent volume distortion.

4

CatBoost Regression

3-tier preparation resolution feeds TF-IDF character n-grams into 5 CatBoost models for instant, verified nutrition totals.
